Label Change Control Workflow

A cross-functional label change process for a medical device / IVD manufacturer selling in many markets. It runs from change initiation through regulatory clearance, print and digital release, and a post-implementation effectiveness check. Risk, usability and design verification are completed before approval, and each market is released only after it is cleared.
