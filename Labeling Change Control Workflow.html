Labeling Change Control Workflow

A cross-functional labeling change process for a pharmaceutical or biotech company marketing small molecules and biologics in many countries. It runs from a safety, CMC or regulatory trigger through the core data sheet, local labeling, health authority approval, artwork and serialization, packaging release and an effectiveness check. Safety and medical review finish before approval, and each market is implemented only after it is approved.
